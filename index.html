Project

Ignition

Ignition is our flagship fleet management platform, created in close collaboration with the UJ Transport & Logistics Department. Built over many months—including the winter season—it brings trip requests, driver and vehicle assignments, and fleet and maintenance analytics together in one system. 🚍

By connecting day-to-day transport operations with clear, useful insights, Ignition turns thoughtful engineering into real-world impact. ⚙️

  • Trip requests and journey coordination
  • Driver and vehicle assignments
  • Maintenance and fleet utilisation analytics
